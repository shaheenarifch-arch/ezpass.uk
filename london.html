Best Time to Visit London with City Pass

Shoulder season (March-May & September-October) is ideal. Crowds are lower, prices are lower, and you can actually get into Westminster Abbey without 1-hour queues. Summer (June-August) is peak - still great but book time slots early via app. Winter (November-February) offers cheapest pass prices and Christmas markets, but some outdoor cruises have reduced schedules. Early morning weekdays always beat weekends for Tower and Abbey.
