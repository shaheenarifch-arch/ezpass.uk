Best Time to Visit Paris With a City Pass

The best pass for Paris works year-round, but timing matters:

  • Shoulder Season (April-May & Sept-Oct): Ideal. Fewer queues, mild weather, all attractions open, best photos. Pass saves most time here as walk-up lines are still 60+ min.
