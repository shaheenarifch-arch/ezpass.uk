Best Time to Visit Venice with a City Pass

Best overall: April to June & September to October. Temperatures are 18-24°C, lines are shorter, and you can actually enjoy the Murano/Burano boat tour. The Venice city pass is especially valuable here because you can use the 7-day validity to spread visits.

Summer (July-August): Busiest and hottest, but the pass saves you the most time. Doge's Palace lines hit 2 hours – skip-the-line alone justifies the pass. Book 2 weeks ahead.

Winter (Nov-Feb): Magical with fewer tourists and Carnevale in February. Some island boats run less frequently, so check meeting times. Passes are cheapest now.

Avoid: Acqua alta (high water) peaks in Nov. Bring waterproof shoe covers – you can still use the pass for indoor museums.
